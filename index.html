About

A visualization studio, by design

AAHO LLC focuses on the media layer of design work: the images, models, films, and presentations that make ideas legible to clients, partners, and audiences.

How we fit into a project

Clients bring the underlying design, product, or development brief. AAHO LLC develops the visualization and presentation media around it — building models, producing renderings and animation, refining imagery, and coordinating deliverables into a clear presentation package.

That includes working from client-provided drawings and CAD/BIM files for visualization purposes, preparing digital assets for rendering or fabrication, and supporting photography and digital imaging where a project needs it.

  • Visualization-first workflow, from input files to final media
  • Still, motion, interactive, and physical presentation formats
  • Production consulting when a project needs coordination across deliverables

Scope & boundaries Please note

AAHO LLC specializes in visualization and presentation media. We do not independently provide licensed architectural or engineering professional services — such as architectural design, structural engineering, code-compliance determinations, or stamped construction documents.

Underlying architectural design and regulatory compliance remain the responsibility of the client and their licensed design professionals. Our role is to visualize, model, and present their work with clarity.
